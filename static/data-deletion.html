Social Glider

Data deletion

Effective October 20, 2026

This page explains how to remove Social Glider's access to your Facebook data and how to have your data deleted. Our Privacy policy lists the data we keep.

If you are a seller

1. Disconnect your Page (optional)

In Social Glider, open Settings › Messenger and click Disconnect. Social Glider stops receiving messages from the Page and deletes the Page's access token. Your conversations, customers and orders stay in your account until you ask us to delete them.

2. Remove Social Glider from Facebook (optional)

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy › Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations (or Apps and websites, if you signed in to Social Glider with Facebook).
  3. Select Social Glider and click Remove.

3. Ask us to delete your data

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from your account's email address, with the subject “Delete my data”. We will delete your account, your shop, your catalog and product photos, and the conversations, customers and orders of your Pages.

If you messaged a seller's Page

If you messaged a Facebook Page that uses Social Glider, you can ask the seller to delete your data, or email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with:

  • the name of the Facebook Page you messaged, and
  • your name as it appears in Messenger.

We will delete your profile, your messages, the photos you sent and your contact details, and let the seller know. The seller may need to keep a record of past orders for their own legal or accounting reasons.

What happens next

  • We may ask you to confirm your request, so we know it comes from you.
  • We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.
  • Deleted data leaves our backups within a further 30 days.
