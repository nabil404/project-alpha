Social Glider

Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Social Glider (“we”, “us”) is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [COUNTRY]. Social Glider helps sellers who run a Facebook Page turn customer conversations in Facebook Messenger into confirmed orders. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. It covers two groups of people:

  • Sellers: people who sign up for Social Glider and connect a Facebook Page.
  • Customers: people who message, or comment on, a seller's Facebook Page while it is connected to Social Glider.

Our role

For seller accounts, we decide how the data is used. For customer data, the seller decides and we process it on the seller's behalf, only to provide Social Glider to that seller. If you are a customer, the seller whose Page you messaged is your first point of contact. You can also contact us directly.

What we collect

From sellers

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a hash) and profile picture. If you sign in with Facebook or Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from that provider.
  • Facebook Page details: the Page's ID and name, and an access token that lets Social Glider act for the Page. We store the token encrypted.
  • Shop information you enter: shop name, contact phone, pickup address, currency, time zone, delivery options and your product catalog, including product photos.
  • Session details: the device, browser and IP address of each signed-in session.

From customers, through Facebook Messenger

  • Your Page-scoped ID (an ID Facebook gives you for that Page only), your name and your profile picture.
  • The messages, photos, reactions and shared posts you send to the Page, and the replies the Page sends you.
  • Order details you give the Page: the products you want, your name, phone number, delivery address and area.
  • Notes the seller adds about you and your orders.

How we use it

  • To run the seller's account and keep it secure.
  • To read customer messages, understand what the customer wants to order, match it to the seller's catalog, reply on the seller's behalf and send an order summary for the customer to confirm.
  • To show the seller their conversations, customers and orders.
  • To send account emails, such as email verification and password resets.
  • To fix problems and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers. We use data received from Facebook only to provide Social Glider to the seller whose Page it came from.

Automated replies

Social Glider uses an AI model to read customer messages and photos and to draft replies and orders. A customer always confirms an order before it is placed, and the seller can pause the assistant and reply personally at any time.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that run Social Glider for us:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, the database and backups.
  • Cloudflare (R2): storage for product photos and photos customers send.
  • OpenAI: processes message text and photos to understand orders. Our agreement does not let OpenAI use this data to train its models.
  • Our email delivery provider, which sends account emails.
  • Meta (Facebook) and Google: sign-in, and sending and receiving Messenger messages.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

How long we keep it

We keep seller data while the account is open and customer data while the seller keeps using Social Glider. When we receive a deletion request, we delete the data within 30 days. Backups are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted data leaves our backups within 30 days after that.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Page access tokens are encrypted at rest, and each seller's data is kept apart from other sellers' data in the database. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights too, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your local data protection authority. To delete your data, follow our data deletion instructions. For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep sellers signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Social Glider is not meant for children under 13, and sellers must be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes
